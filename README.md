# Andrea Saliola Bucelli

Hi, I'm Andrea.

I am an MSc Finance graduate from LUISS Guido Carli, with international experience at EDHEC Business School and the Technical University of Munich.

I am interested in quantitative finance, statistical modeling, and applied machine learning, with a particular focus on predictive modeling, market regimes, risk, and asset allocation.

My projects focus on:

- Financial econometrics and time series
- Machine learning for financial markets (and beyond)
- Risk modeling and portfolio analysis
- Deep learning and network methods
- Reproducible empirical research

## Projects

[**Institutional Crowding and Equity Fragility**](https://github.com/ansabu01/institutional-crowding-equity-fragility)  
Predicting stock-level downside risk using market information, institutional ownership, gradient-boosted models, and graph neural networks.

[**Market-Stress Regimes and Diversification Breakdown**](https://github.com/ansabu01/market-stress-regimes)  
Examining how correlations and risk concentration change between calm and stressed markets using regime classification, bootstrap inference, principal-component analysis, and volatility-adjusted correlations.

[**Urban Heat-Risk Drivers in Europe**](https://github.com/ansabu01/urban-heat-risk-drivers-europe)  
Interpretable analysis of European street-level sensor data to identify the environmental and urban characteristics associated with heat risk.

[**Linear vs. Kernel Models in Simulated Returns**](https://github.com/ansabu01/Linear_vs_Kernel_Models_in_Simulated_Returns)  
Simulation study comparing linear regression and kernel ridge regression under nonlinear and regime-switching return processes.
